# Commercial Agent-Training Licensing

History Forge offers separately negotiated licenses for commercial agent and
model training using data expressly designated under the History Forge Data
License.

A commercial license must identify the exact package and hashes, permitted
activities, model or agent scope, evaluation versus training rights, deployment
and model-distribution rights, term, territory, users or affiliates, provenance
obligations, support and commercial terms. Evaluation access alone does not
authorize training, persistent optimization, customer redistribution or
production deployment.

An agreement does not take away independent rights in Apache-licensed software,
earlier public data grants, public-domain material, third-party licenses or uses
allowed by applicable law. It grants only the rights Forge controls in the
identified package.

This page is information, not an offer, quote or license grant. A commercial
license exists only in a completed written agreement approved by an authorized
representative of Titans Forge LLC. Contact Titans Forge through the public
organization page at <https://github.com/titans-forge> to begin a discussion.
