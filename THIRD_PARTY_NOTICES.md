# Third-Party Notices

## Historical Text

Only historical signature-list text is reproduced. These eighteenth-century names, state headings and subscription labels are public domain. No claim is made that every item on the National Archives website is public domain.

- [Declaration transcription](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/declaration-transcript): NARA transcription of the Stone engraving of the parchment Declaration. Adoption date July 4, 1776 is not an individual signing date.
- [Constitution transcription](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/constitution-transcript): NARA transcription of the parchment Constitution inscribed by Jacob Shallus. Document date September 17, 1787. Secretary William Jackson is excluded from delegate-signature edges.
- Identity references: the transcription's links to NARA's [Pennsylvania](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/founding-fathers-pennsylvania), [Connecticut](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/founding-fathers-connecticut) and [Delaware](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/founding-fathers-delaware) entries. Only names, headings, URLs and hashes are included, not the biographical essays or illustrations.
- [NARA copyright and use policy](https://www.archives.gov/global-pages/privacy.html) distinguishes NARA-produced government works from other collection material. This release relies on the historical signature text's public-domain status, not a blanket clearance of the website.

Raw capture SHA-256 values and explicit identity references are preserved in `public/data/source-inputs.json`. Full HTML captures, logos, seals, images, modern interpretive essays and biographies are not distributed. No NARA or U.S. Government endorsement is implied.
